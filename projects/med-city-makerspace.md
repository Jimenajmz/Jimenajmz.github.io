After learning that Rochester did not have a makerspace like the one I had worked at during university, I decided that I wanted to help start one if I moved to the area. When I arrived in Rochester, I began connecting with other community members who wanted a makerspace, too. I soon met Dave Razidlo, and things began moving quickly. Since our first meeting, we have continued meeting weekly and have found more people who share our vision. We do not have a physical space yet, but we are spreading the word, building community, and moving closer to making the space a reality.
