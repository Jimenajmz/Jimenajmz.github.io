I have been making arts and crafts for as long as I can remember. Eventually, you have to find something to do with all the things you make, whether that means throwing them away or, more enjoyably, giving them away or selling them. I first sold my work in elementary school, when I made Rainbow Loom bracelets and sold them to other kids for quarters. In middle school, a friend introduced me to the Uptown Art Fair Youth Craft Fair, where I sold handmade sewn plushies, pins, and art prints for $10 each. In high school, I started selling crocheted plushies and taking illustration and crochet commissions. In college, I returned to selling by making silly little earrings with the campus makerspace. I sold them at friends' DIY events and parties. Now, I plan to sell my work occasionally as a hobby, whenever I feel like it.
