## Ingredients

**Sauce**

- 1 can of tomatoes (14.5 oz)
- 2 jalapeños
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 tsp chicken bouillon
- 1/4 onion
- 2-3 garlic cloves
- A few sprigs of epazote

**Chips**

- 8 corn tortillas
- Oil, for frying
- Salt

**To serve**

- Eggs

## Steps

**The chips**

1. Cut the tortillas into chip-sized triangles.
2. Heat about 1/2 inch of oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Fry the tortilla pieces in batches for 1 to 2 minutes per side, until golden and crisp.
3. Drain the chips on paper towels and sprinkle them with salt.

**The sauce**

1. Blend the tomatoes, jalapeños, water, chicken bouillon, onion and garlic until smooth.
2. Pour the sauce into a pot, add the epazote, and bring it to a boil.
3. Turn the heat down and simmer for 10 minutes, until the sauce thickens a little.

**Putting it together**

1. Add the chips to the sauce and stir for about a minute, until they are coated but still a little crisp.
2. Fry an egg for each serving and put it on top. Eat right away.
