## Ingredients

**Tilapia**

- 4 tilapia fillets
- 1 Tbsp butter
- 1 Tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 Roma tomatoes
- 1 jalapeño pepper
- 1 serrano pepper
- 1 onion
- A bunch of cilantro
- 2 limes, squeezed (about 1/4 cup of juice)
- Adobo, to taste

**Seasonings**

- Garlic powder
- Onion powder
- Paprika
- Oregano

**Rice**

- Rice
- Water
- Vegetable oil
- Onion
- Garlic
- Cilantro
- Salt

**Beans**

- Black beans
- Vegetable oil
- Onion
- Garlic
- Tomato
- Jalapeños
- Red pepper
- Oregano
- Onion powder
- Garlic powder
- Adobo

## Steps

**The tilapia**

1. Dice the Roma tomatoes, jalapeño pepper, serrano pepper, onion and cilantro.
2. Season the tilapia with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika and oregano.
3. Melt the butter in a pan and cook the tilapia for 2 minutes on each side. Set it aside.
4. In a different pot, heat the oil, add all the veggies, and sauté for 2 to 3 minutes. Season with the same seasonings.
5. Add the squeezed limes and simmer for 3 to 4 minutes. Once cooked, taste and add adobo.
6. Add the tilapia back in, cover, and turn the heat to low for 5 additional minutes.

**The rice**

1. Put oil at the bottom of a pot and heat it up.
2. Throw in finely chopped onion and garlic.
3. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes.
4. Add the rice and cook it for a bit.
5. Add twice the amount of water as rice, and fresh cilantro.
6. Add salt.
7. Boil on high until you see bubbles from the rice and there is no more water.
8. Turn the heat to low, let it rest, and stir.
9. Cover for 10 minutes.
10. Then uncover and wait another 10 minutes.

**The beans**

1. Mince the garlic, and dice the onion, tomato and jalapeños.
2. Put the onion and garlic on the stove until the onion becomes translucent.
3. Add the tomato and jalapeños.
4. When the tomato and jalapeños soften, add the beans (try to add only half the bean juice).
5. Season with red pepper, oregano, onion powder, garlic powder and adobo.
6. Cook for 10 minutes.

Serve the tilapia over the rice, with the beans on the side.
