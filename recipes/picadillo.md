## Ingredients

**Picadillo**

- 1 Tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 Tbsp butter
- 1 small onion, peeled and chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, peeled and minced
- 1.5 pounds ground beef
- 1 cup tomato sauce
- 1.5 cups water
- 1 chicken bouillon cube (the water and bouillon make the chicken broth)
- 2 medium potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 2 medium carrots, peeled and cubed
- 2 Tbsp chopped cilantro
- 1/2 cup green peas, thawed
- 1/4 cup green olives
- Salt and pepper, to taste

**Seasonings (1.5 tsp of each)**

- Adobo
- Oregano
- Cumin

**Rice**

- Rice
- Water
- Vegetable oil
- Onion
- Garlic
- Cilantro
- Salt

**Beans**

- Black beans
- Vegetable oil
- Onion
- Garlic
- Tomato
- Jalapeños
- Red pepper
- Oregano
- Onion powder
- Garlic powder
- Adobo

## Steps

**The picadillo**

1. In a pan over medium heat, heat the oil, add the onion and garlic, and cook until softened.
2. Melt the butter in the pan.
3. Add the ground beef and seasonings and cook, breaking the beef into pieces with the back of a spoon, until lightly browned.
4. Add the tomato sauce.
5. Add the water and chicken bouillon and bring to a boil. Lower the heat, cover, and cook for about 10 to 15 minutes, or until the beef is cooked through.
6. Add the potatoes, carrots and cilantro, cover, and continue to simmer for about 3 to 5 minutes, or until tender.
7. Add the green peas and olives.
8. Continue to cook until the sauce is reduced and thickened. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

**The rice**

1. Put oil at the bottom of a pot and heat it up.
2. Throw in finely chopped onion and garlic.
3. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes.
4. Add the rice and cook it for a bit.
5. Add twice the amount of water as rice, and fresh cilantro.
6. Add salt.
7. Boil on high until you see bubbles from the rice and there is no more water.
8. Turn the heat to low, let it rest, and stir.
9. Cover for 10 minutes.
10. Then uncover and wait another 10 minutes.

**The beans**

1. Mince the garlic, and dice the onion, tomato and jalapeños.
2. Put the onion and garlic on the stove until the onion becomes translucent.
3. Add the tomato and jalapeños.
4. When the tomato and jalapeños soften, add the beans (try to add only half the bean juice).
5. Season with red pepper, oregano, onion powder, garlic powder and adobo.
6. Cook for 10 minutes.

Serve the picadillo over the rice, with the beans on the side.
