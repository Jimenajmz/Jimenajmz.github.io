---
title: Sun-Dried Tomato Chicken Pasta
category: Entrees
---
## Ingredients

**Pasta**

- Pasta
- Salt, a generous amount for the water

**Sauce**

- Oil
- Garlic, chopped
- Sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
- Tomato paste
- Italian seasoning
- Garlic powder
- Black pepper
- Heavy whipping cream
- Cheese
- Pasta water

**Chicken**

- Chicken
- Oil from the sun-dried tomato jar
- Garlic
- Paprika
- Italian seasoning
- Pepper flakes
- Parmesan cheese

## Steps

**Making the pasta**

1. Boil water with a generous amount of salt.

**For the pasta sauce**

1. Put oil and chopped garlic in a pot.
2. Add chopped sun-dried tomatoes, tomato paste, Italian seasoning, garlic powder and black pepper.
3. Add heavy whipping cream.
4. Bring to a boil.
5. Add cheese.
6. Add pasta water until it reaches your desired consistency.

**For the chicken**

1. Dice the chicken into cubes.
2. Toss it in a pan with oil from the sun-dried tomato jar and garlic.
3. Toss in a shit ton of paprika, Italian seasoning and pepper flakes.
4. Cook, then add Parmesan cheese.
