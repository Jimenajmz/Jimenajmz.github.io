---
title: Green Chicken Enchiladas
category: Entrees
---
## Ingredients

**Chicken**

- 3 pounds of chicken
- 1/4 onion
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 Tbsp salt

**Sauce**

- 1 lb tomatillos
- 1/4 onion
- 2 large garlic cloves
- 3 serrano chiles
- 1 poblano chile
- 1.5 cups cooked chicken broth
- 2 Tbsp chicken bouillon
- Splash of heavy whipping cream

**To assemble**

- Tortillas
- Oil, for softening the tortillas
- Onion
- Mozzarella cheese

## Steps

**The chicken**

1. Boil the chicken with the onion, garlic and salt for 40 minutes, then shred it.

**The sauce**

1. Boil the tomatillos, onion, garlic, serrano chiles and poblano chile.
2. Blend them with the chicken broth, chicken bouillon and a splash of heavy whipping cream.
3. Fry and stir the sauce for 10 minutes.

**Putting it together**

1. Soften the tortillas in oil, dip them in the sauce, add the chicken and onion, roll them up, then add cheese.
2. Put them in a pan, top with mozzarella, and bake at 400°F for 12 to 15 minutes.
