## Ingredients

- 2 green plantains
- Oil, for frying
- 2 Tbsp butter, melted
- Salt

## Steps

1. Peel the plantains and cut them into chunks about 1 inch thick. To peel, cut off both ends, make a slit down the skin, and pry it off.
2. Heat about 1 inch of oil in a deep pan over medium-high heat. Fry the plantain chunks for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until light golden.
3. Take the plantains out and drain them on paper towels.
4. While they are still warm, smash each chunk flat with a plate or a cutting board, to about 1/2 inch thick.
5. Fry them again for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until golden and crisp.
6. Drain them, brush with the butter, and sprinkle with salt. Serve hot.
