## Ingredients

**Chicken**

- 3 pounds of chicken
- 1/4 onion
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 Tbsp salt
- Water, to cover

**Sauce**

- 1 lb tomatillos
- 1/4 onion
- 2 large garlic cloves
- 3 serrano chiles
- 1 poblano chile
- 1.5 cups cooked chicken broth
- 2 Tbsp chicken bouillon
- Splash of heavy whipping cream

**To assemble**

- 12 corn tortillas
- 1/2 cup oil, for softening the tortillas
- 1/2 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

## Steps

**The chicken**

1. Put the chicken, onion, garlic and salt in a large pot and cover with water.
2. Boil for 40 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
3. Take the chicken out of the pot and shred it with two forks. Keep the broth for the sauce.

**The sauce**

1. Put the tomatillos, onion, garlic, serrano chiles and poblano chile in a pot, cover with water, and boil for about 10 minutes, until the tomatillos turn dull green and soft.
2. Drain them and put them in a blender with the chicken broth, chicken bouillon and a splash of heavy whipping cream. Blend until smooth.
3. Pour the sauce into a pan and fry and stir it for 10 minutes, until it thickens a little.

**Putting it together**

1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
2. Heat the oil in a small pan. Soften each tortilla in the oil for a few seconds per side, then dip it in the sauce.
3. Fill each tortilla with shredded chicken and a little chopped onion, then roll it up.
4. Lay the enchiladas seam side down in a baking dish, close together. Pour the rest of the sauce on top.
5. Top with the mozzarella and bake at 400°F for 12 to 15 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbling.
