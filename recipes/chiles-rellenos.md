Cook the rice separately from the beans.

## Ingredients

- 8 poblano chiles
- 2 cups rice
- 4 cups water
- 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
- 1 can of black beans (15 oz)
- 1 yellow onion
- 4 jalapeños
- 1 tomato
- 2 Tbsp tomato paste (or a small amount of ketchup)
- 8 oz queso fresco
- 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 Tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 1/2 tsp salt

**Seasoning**

- Red pepper
- Oregano
- Onion powder
- Adobo
- Garlic powder

## Steps

**The rice**

1. Put 2 Tbsp of the oil at the bottom of a pot and heat it up.
2. Throw in half of the onion, finely chopped, and half of the garlic.
3. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes.
4. Add the rice and cook it for a bit, stirring, until it smells toasty.
5. Add the water (twice the amount of the rice) and half of the cilantro.
6. Add the salt.
7. Boil on high until you see bubbles from the rice and there is no more water on top.
8. Turn the heat to low, let it rest, and stir once.
9. Cover for 10 minutes.
10. Then uncover and wait another 10 minutes. Fluff with a fork.

**The beans and mixture**

1. Mince the rest of the garlic, and dice the rest of the onion, the tomato and the jalapeños.
2. Heat the last Tbsp of oil in a pan. Cook the onion and garlic until the onion becomes translucent.
3. Add the tomato and jalapeños.
4. When the tomato and jalapeños soften, stir in the tomato paste (or ketchup) and cook for a minute or two to finish the sofrito.
5. Add the beans (try to add only half the bean juice).
6. Season with the red pepper, oregano, onion powder, garlic powder and adobo.
7. Cook for 10 minutes, then stir in the rest of the cilantro.

**The chiles**

1. Roast the chiles over a gas flame, under the broiler, or on a grill, turning them until they are black all over.
2. Put the chiles in a bag, or in a covered bowl, for about 10 minutes to sweat.
3. Peel off the burnt skin. Cut a slit down the side of each chile and remove the seeds, leaving the stem on.

**Finishing touches**

1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
2. Mix the beans and the rice.
3. Fill the inside of each poblano with queso fresco, mozzarella cheese and the beans-and-rice mixture. Set them in a baking dish.
4. Bake for 10 to 15 minutes, until the cheese melts.
5. Serve.
